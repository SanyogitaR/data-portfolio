About Me

Hey! I'm a pre-final year Computer Science student at VIT Bhopal University with a deep passion for data analysis and data science. I spend my days diving into complex datasets to uncover hidden patterns, stories, and actionable insights that can drive meaningful business decisions. What excites me most is the art of transforming raw, messy data into clear, compelling narratives. Whether it's crafting predictive models that forecast trends, performing deep statistical analysis to reveal correlations, or developing sophisticated algorithms to solve real-world problems - I thrive on finding the signal in the noise. My journey in data has taken me through exploratory data analysis, machine learning model development, A/B testing frameworks, and building production-ready data pipelines. I'm constantly experimenting with new techniques to extract maximum value from every dataset. When I'm not working with data, you'll find me designing intuitive UI/UX interfaces, contributing to open-source projects, or reading the latest research papers on AI and machine learning. Writing helps me solidify my understanding of complex concepts, while reading sci-fi novels fuels my imagination for what's possible with technology.
